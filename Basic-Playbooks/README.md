# Incident Response Playbooks

Incident response playbooks, procedures, templates, and operational guidance for cybersecurity incident management.

## 📝 Overview

This directory contains incident response resources and playbooks designed to:

- Establish standardized incident response procedures
- Provide step-by-step guidance for security teams
- Support rapid and effective incident handling
- Enable consistent communication during security events
- Facilitate post-incident reviews and learning

- Root cause analysis (RCA)

## 📋 Playbook Structure

Each playbook includes:

1. **Incident Type & Severity** - Clear definition and classification criteria
2. **Detection & Validation** - How to identify and confirm the incident
3. **Initial Response** - Immediate actions and notifications
4. **Investigation Steps** - Detailed investigation procedures
5. **Containment Actions** - Steps to limit impact and scope
6. **Recovery Procedures** - Restoration and remediation steps
7. **Communication Plan** - Notification templates and procedures
8. **Post-Incident** - Review, reporting, and improvement actions
9. **Tools & Resources** - Required tools, contacts, and references

## 🚀 Implementation

To use these playbooks:

1. **Tailor to Your Organization**
   - Customize with your specific systems and contacts
   - Update with your organization's escalation procedures
   - Include your incident response team details

2. **Regular Review & Testing**
   - Review playbooks quarterly
   - Test procedures through tabletop exercises
   - Update based on lessons learned

3. **Training & Awareness**
   - Ensure team familiarity with procedures
   - Conduct regular training sessions
   - Practice through simulations and drills

4. **Continuous Improvement**
   - Document incidents and responses
   - Gather feedback from incidents
   - Update playbooks based on new threats
   - Share learnings across the team
