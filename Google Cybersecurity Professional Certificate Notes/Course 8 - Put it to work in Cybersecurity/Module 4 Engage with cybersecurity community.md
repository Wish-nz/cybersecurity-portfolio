## Helpful cybersecurity resources

Staying Up-to-Date in Cybersecurity

- The cybersecurity industry is constantly changing, as exemplified by the OWASP Top 10, which is updated every three to four years to reflect new risks.
- Continuing your education beyond this certificate program is crucial for career advancement and demonstrates your commitment to hiring managers.

Recommended Security Resources

- **CSO Online:** Offers news, analysis, and research on various security and risk management topics, serving as a valuable resource for cybersecurity professionals.
- **Krebs on Security:** An in-depth blog by Brian Krebs that covers security news and investigations into cyberattacks, helping you stay informed about global security events.
- **Dark Reading:** A popular website for security professionals, providing information on diverse security topics such as analytics, application security, mobile and cloud security, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

## Strategies for engaging with the cybersecurity community

Security Organizations and Conferences

- Attending security conferences and joining organizations allows you to gain knowledge from seasoned professionals and stay updated on security strategies.
- It's important to identify your specific interests in security (e.g., incident response, forensic security) before searching for relevant organizations or conferences.

Finding Resources

- Conduct web searches using specific keywords related to your security interests to find local organizations or conferences.
- Utilize social media platforms like LinkedIn to discover security groups or organizations, but be mindful of social engineering tactics.

Mailing Lists and Key Takeaways

- Sign up for cybersecurity mailing lists, such as those offered by CISA, to receive periodic updates on various security topics and new vulnerabilities.
- Staying connected with the cybersecurity community through these avenues is crucial as attackers constantly develop new ways to compromise data.

## Engage with the cybersecurity community in a meaningful way

Using Social Media for Professional Networking

- **Follow Industry Leaders:** Connect with Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) on platforms like LinkedIn to gain insights from their posts, interviews, and shared articles.
- **Connect with Security Analysts:** Search for cybersecurity analysts on LinkedIn and send connection requests with a brief, personalized message to learn about their experiences.

Alternative Networking Strategies

- **Join Security Associations:** For those less active on social media, joining cybersecurity industry associations is an effective way to connect with other professionals and find mentors.
- **Research Associations:** Use internet search engines to find cybersecurity industry associations that align with your professional goals.

## Connect with other cybersecurity professionals

Connecting with CISOs

- Following Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) on platforms like LinkedIn® provides access to their shared insights, trends, and news in the security world.
- This practice helps you stay informed and sharpens your security mindset, which is crucial for career progression.

Networking with Other Security Professionals

- LinkedIn® is a valuable tool for connecting with other security professionals, from entry-level analysts to seasoned experts.
- When sending connection requests, craft a clear, conversational message that states your intentions and avoids any impression of being a scammer.
