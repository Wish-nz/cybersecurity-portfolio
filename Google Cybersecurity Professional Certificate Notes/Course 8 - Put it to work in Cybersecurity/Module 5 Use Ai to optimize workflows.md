## Introduction to AI in Cybersecurity

AI in Cybersecurity

- AI tools can augment and automate general work tasks, such as drafting emails and documents, summarizing information, and helping to analyze data.
- Cybersecurity professionals are already using AI to automate routine tasks, enhance productivity, and strengthen defenses against cyber attacks.

The Dual Nature of AI

- While AI is a powerful ally, its increasing prevalence makes AI systems attractive targets for malicious actors.
- Cybercriminals can leverage AI to launch more sophisticated attacks, evade detection, and exploit system weaknesses.

Your Role as a Cybersecurity Professional

- Understanding AI's potential for both good and harm is crucial for cybersecurity professionals.
- You will need to master the tools and techniques to secure AI systems and stay ahead of those who seek to misuse them.

## Driving impact with AI in the workplace

AI as a Productivity Multiplier

- AI significantly enhances efficiency in cybersecurity, making individuals and teams faster and more productive.
- It helps in understanding complex concepts and automating tedious tasks, such as revising lengthy documents.

Practical Applications and Ethical Considerations of AI

- AI tools can process extensive documents, allowing teams to quickly extract necessary information and avoid sifting through irrelevant content.
- It is crucial to verify AI outputs, often through a "human-in-the-loop" approach, by having colleagues review the generated information.

## Use generative AI to work smarter and faster

Understanding AI and Generative AI

- AI refers to computer programs that perform cognitive tasks similar to human intelligence.
- Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content, such as text, images, or other media, based on prompts.

Applications of Generative AI in Cybersecurity

- **Content Creation:** Generative AI can be used to create fake data for testing cybersecurity tools.
- **Information Analysis:** It can quickly summarize large amounts of information, like reports or meeting transcripts, to identify key security details.

Enhancing Efficiency and Research

- **Answering Questions:** Generative AI can provide detailed and nuanced answers, making it useful for researching cybersecurity threats like malware and ransomware.
- **Streamlining Daily Tasks:** AI tools can assist with routine tasks, such as providing an initial analysis of potentially malicious emails.

## Boost your cybersecurity skills with AI

AI's Role in Cybersecurity

- AI helps cybersecurity professionals understand complex security frameworks, identify vulnerabilities, and streamline workflows.
- It allows for quicker completion of routine tasks, freeing up time for more critical problem-solving and strategic initiatives.

The T-C-R-E-I Prompting Framework

- **Task:** Clearly define what you want the AI model to do, including specifying a persona (e.g., a security expert) and the desired output format (e.g., bulleted list).
- **Context & References:** Provide necessary details and examples to help the AI understand your needs and generate more useful and relevant outputs.

Responsible AI Usage

- AI should complement human skills, not replace them, requiring a "human-in-the-loop" approach for verification and refinement of AI-generated results.
- Always be mindful of the information you input into AI tools, avoiding confidential or sensitive data, and always verify the output.

## Decode complex security frameworks with AI

The Challenge of Security Frameworks

- Security frameworks, such as NIST Special Publication 800-53, are crucial for mitigating risks and threats to data and privacy, but they can be extensive and complex.
- Understanding specific controls and their nuances within these lengthy documents can be time-consuming for security professionals.

How AI Can Help

- Generative AI tools can provide deeper insights into security controls, offering explanations, implementation guidance, and enhancement suggestions.
- By providing context in prompts, such as whether a system is federal or non-federal, AI can tailor responses to specific needs and indicate if enhancements are optional or required.

Practical Application and Customization

- AI tools can explain controls like SI-5 (System Integrity) by detailing what they entail, how to implement them, and relevant enhancements.
- Users can customize prompts to receive explanations in different styles, such as simpler language for new analysts or real-world examples, to better grasp complex topics.

## Use AI to help identify bugs

The Value of Coding in Cybersecurity

- Writing code helps security professionals automate repetitive tasks, improving accuracy and efficiency while reducing human error.
- Even experienced programmers can introduce bugs, and finding these errors manually can be time-consuming and frustrating.

Generative AI as a Code Reviewer

- Generative AI tools can act as code reviewers, quickly identifying bugs that might be overlooked by human eyes.
- These tools can detect issues like zero division errors, which occur when a program attempts to divide by zero, potentially causing system failures.

Enhancing Code Robustness and Security

- Beyond identifying bugs, generative AI can suggest solutions, such as adding conditional statements to handle edge cases gracefully.
- By catching unknown bugs and vulnerabilities, these tools help maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems, even in seemingly functional code.

## Refine code with help from AI

Enhancing Code with Generative AI

- Generative AI tools can add comments to existing code, explaining what each section does, which is particularly helpful for those with limited programming experience.
- These tools can also suggest improvements for existing code, offering actionable ideas and explanations to guide further development.

Expanding AI's Role in Coding

- Generative AI is versatile and can assist with various programming languages, not just Python.
- Beyond improving existing code, generative AI can also help users start writing programs from scratch, saving time and increasing productivity.

## Understand system vulnerabilities with AI

Understanding and Assessing Vulnerabilities with AI

- Generative AI tools can define vulnerabilities, identify their potential impact on systems, and suggest immediate mitigation steps.
- By providing context, such as identifying yourself as a junior cybersecurity analyst, you can help the AI tailor its response to your skill level.

Iterating and Refining AI Prompts

- The "evaluate" (E) and "iterate" (I) steps of the T-C-R-E-I prompting framework are crucial for getting accurate and useful results.
- Tips for iteration include providing more specific guidance, offering examples of expected output, clarifying phrasing, and highlighting preferred parts of the AI's response.

Verifying Information and Interview Preparation

- Always verify information provided by generative AI tools against reliable sources, as AI outputs can sometimes be inaccurate or outdated.
- Generative AI can also be used to prepare for interviews by generating sample questions and providing answers for comparison.


## Prioritize alerts with AI

AI as a Troubleshooting Assistant

- AI tools like Gemini can help security analysts prioritize and investigate intrusion detection system (IDS) alerts by reviewing alert information and providing explanations for prioritization.
- AI can also offer additional considerations, such as checking for correlations between alerts and recommending a well-defined incident response plan.

Enhancing Incident Response Plans

- Generative AI can be used to identify appropriate responses for incidents not covered in an existing incident response plan.
- It can also help document actions for new alert types or vulnerabilities that are missing from the plan.

Experimentation and Workflow Optimization

- Experimenting with prompts is crucial to effectively leverage AI tools for cybersecurity tasks.
- AI can streamline various workflows, from threat detection to code updates, by assisting with tasks that typically consume a lot of time.
