Understanding Information vs. Intelligence

- **Information** refers to raw data or facts about a subject, such as a new operating system update.
- **Intelligence** is the result of analyzing information to gain insights and knowledge for decision-making, like determining whether to install an OS update based on linked cyber threats.

The Role of OSINT in Cybersecurity

- OSINT helps organizations gain insights into potential threats and vulnerabilities, similar to how businesses use customer insights.
- It is crucial for Information Security (InfoSec) teams to protect networks by monitoring online discussions for emerging vulnerabilities and prioritizing protective measures.

OSINT Tools and Resources

- Various sources like search engines, social media, and forums can be used to gather OSINT.
- Tools such as VirusTotal, MITRE ATT&CK®, OSINT Framework, and Have I been Pwned assist in collecting and analyzing open-source information.
