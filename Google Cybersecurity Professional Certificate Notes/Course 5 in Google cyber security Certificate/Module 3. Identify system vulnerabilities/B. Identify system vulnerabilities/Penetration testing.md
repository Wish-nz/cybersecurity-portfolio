Penetration Testing Explained

- A penetration test (pen test) is a simulated cyberattack designed to identify vulnerabilities in systems, networks, applications, and processes.
- Unlike vulnerability assessments that merely find weaknesses, pen tests exploit these weaknesses to understand the potential impact of a real attack.

Approaches to Penetration Testing

- **Red team tests** simulate attacks to find vulnerabilities.
- **Blue team tests** focus on defense and incident response.
- **Purple team tests** combine elements of both red and blue teams for comprehensive security improvement.

Penetration Testing Strategies

- **Open-box testing** (white-box, clear-box) involves the tester having full knowledge and access to internal systems.
- **Closed-box testing** (black-box, zero knowledge) means the tester has little to no prior information, mimicking a malicious hacker.
- **Partial knowledge testing** (gray-box) provides the tester with limited access and information about the system.
