The Importance of Updates

- Updates are crucial for addressing security vulnerabilities that can put users, devices, and networks at risk.
- They act like "locking doors" to prevent malicious actors from exploiting gaps in security, often containing bug fixes for common vulnerabilities.

Update Strategies

- **Manual Updates:** These require IT departments or users to obtain and install updates. While offering control, a disadvantage is the risk of critical updates being overlooked.
- **Automatic Updates:** The system or application handles finding, downloading, and installing updates. This simplifies deployment and keeps systems current, but poorly tested patches can lead to instability.

End-of-Life (EOL) Software

- EOL software is no longer supported by manufacturers, posing an unfixable risk as no new updates or patches are released.
- Despite recommendations to discontinue use, replacing EOL technology can be costly, leading to continued use and increased risk, especially with the growing number of connected devices.
