The Attacker Mindset

- Applying an attacker mindset involves simulating problems in a controlled environment to gain insights into potential vulnerabilities.
- This perspective helps in establishing or modifying security plans by understanding how attackers might exploit weaknesses.

Simulating Threats

- Attack simulations are conducted proactively (red team exercises) to exploit vulnerabilities or reactively (blue team exercises) to respond to attacks.
- Proactive teams plan attacks to evaluate security awareness, while reactive teams gather information and use vulnerability scanning tools to identify weaknesses.

Scanning for Vulnerabilities

- Vulnerability scanners automatically compare existing common vulnerabilities against network technologies to uncover weaknesses.
- The results of these scans are crucial for reactive simulations to assess risks and determine remediation strategies, following steps like identification, analysis, risk assessment, and remediation.
