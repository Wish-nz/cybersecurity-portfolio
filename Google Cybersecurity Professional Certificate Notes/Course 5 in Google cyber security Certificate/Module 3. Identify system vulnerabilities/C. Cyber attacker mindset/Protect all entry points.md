Understanding the Attack Surface

- An attack surface encompasses all possible points where a threat actor could exploit vulnerabilities within a system or organization.
- Analyzing the attack surface is a crucial first step for security teams to effectively allocate resources for defense.

Physical Attack Surface

- The physical attack surface involves people and their devices, and it can be attacked from both internal and external sources.
- Security hardening, which involves strengthening a system to reduce vulnerabilities and limit entry points, is essential for minimizing the physical attack surface.

Digital Attack Surface

- The digital attack surface includes everything beyond an organization's firewall, such as online connections and cloud-stored information.
- Cloud computing has significantly expanded the digital attack surface, making it more challenging for organizations to defend against threats from various entry points.
