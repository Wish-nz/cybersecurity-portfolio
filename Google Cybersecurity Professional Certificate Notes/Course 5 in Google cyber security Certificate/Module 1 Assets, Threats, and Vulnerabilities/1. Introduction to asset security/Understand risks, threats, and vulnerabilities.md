Understanding Risk in Cybersecurity

- Risk is defined as anything that can impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset, and its interpretation can vary across organizations.
- Risk is often calculated as "Likelihood x Impact," helping organizations prevent costly events, identify system improvements, and prioritize critical assets.

Threats and Their Categories

- Threats are circumstances or events that can negatively impact assets, such as a nail puncturing a tire for a car.
- Threats are commonly categorized as either intentional (e.g., a malicious hacker) or unintentional (e.g., an employee holding a door open for an unknown person).

Vulnerabilities and Their Categories

- Vulnerabilities are weaknesses that can be exploited by threats, like a tire being vulnerable to sharp objects.
- Vulnerabilities can be grouped into technical (e.g., misconfigured software) and human (e.g., a forgetful employee losing an access card) categories.
