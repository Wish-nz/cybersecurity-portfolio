Importance of Asset Management

- Asset management is crucial for tracking assets and their associated risks, ensuring that organizations can protect what they possess.
- It involves identifying, tracking, and classifying various assets, including digital data, information systems, physical equipment, and intangible assets like brand reputation.

Asset Classification and Its Benefits

- Asset classification involves labeling assets based on their sensitivity and importance, which helps in determining their value and the level of protection required.
- This practice aids in effective risk management, prioritizing security resources, reducing IT costs, and maintaining compliance with legal regulations.

Common Classification Levels and Challenges

- The most common classification scheme includes restricted, confidential, internal-only, and public, with "restricted" being the highest level for highly sensitive information.
- Challenges in classifying assets, especially information, include identifying the true owner and dealing with assets that may have multiple classification values simultaneously.
