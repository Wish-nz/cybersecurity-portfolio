The Importance of a Security Culture

- Security is a shared set of values that spans all levels of an organization, involving employees, vendors, and customers.
- Protecting digital and physical assets requires everyone's participation, which is why security plans are crucial.

Understanding Risks and Factors

- Risks are anything that can impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset.
- Common risk categories include damage, disclosure, or loss of information, which can be caused by factors like physical damage, malfunctions, attacks, or human error.

The Three Elements of Security Plans

- **Policies:** These are foundational rules that reduce risk and protect information, outlining the scope, objectives, and limitations of a security plan.
- **Standards:** These provide tactical guidance on how to protect assets, acting as references for setting policies (e.g., NIST Special Publication 800-63B for password management).
- **Procedures:** These are step-by-step instructions for performing specific security tasks, ensuring accountability, consistency, and efficiency.
