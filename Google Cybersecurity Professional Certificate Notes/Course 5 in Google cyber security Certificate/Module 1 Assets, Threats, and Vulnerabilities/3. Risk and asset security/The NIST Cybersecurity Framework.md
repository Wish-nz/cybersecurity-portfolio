Understanding Security Compliance

- Compliance involves adhering to internal standards and external regulations to protect assets, maintain trust, and avoid penalties.
- Regulations are rules set by authorities to protect people and information on a larger scale, especially crucial in highly regulated industries.

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

- The NIST CSF is a voluntary framework offering standards, guidelines, and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk, focusing on securing information.
- It comprises three main components: the Core, Tiers, and Profiles, which together provide a comprehensive approach to cybersecurity.

Components of the NIST CSF

- **The Core:** Identifies five key functions of a security plan: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover, acting as a security checklist.
- **Tiers:** Measure performance across the five core functions, ranging from Level 1 (bare minimum) to Level 4 (exemplary), showing organizations what aspects of their security plans are effective.
- **Profiles:** Offer insight into the current state of a security plan, similar to snapshots in time, allowing for comparison and identification of changes or improvements.
