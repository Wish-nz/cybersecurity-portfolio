The Value of Data

- **Data as a primary asset:** In today's connected world, information, particularly in digital form, is a crucial asset that security teams must protect.
- **Information Security (InfoSec):** This practice focuses on safeguarding data in all its states from unauthorized access to prevent issues like identity theft, financial loss, and reputational damage.

Three States of Data Protection

- **Data in Use:** This refers to data actively being accessed by users, such as checking emails on a laptop.
- **Data in Transit:** This describes data moving from one location to another, like sending an email.
- **Data at Rest:** This is data that is stored on a physical device and not currently being accessed, such as when a laptop is closed.

Evolving Security Mindset

- **Dynamic nature of data at rest:** With the rise of cloud storage, data on devices like smartphones may not truly be "at rest" even when the device is inactive, requiring a constant awareness of new vulnerabilities.
- **Connecting to asset management:** Understanding these data states is crucial for security teams to analyze risks and develop effective asset management plans.
