Information Security vs. Information Privacy

- **Information privacy** focuses on an individual's right to control how their personal data is collected and used, including unauthorized access and distribution.
- **Information security (InfoSec)** involves the practices and safeguards implemented to protect data in all its states from unauthorized users and potential threats.

Why Privacy Matters in Security

- The increasing collection and use of personal data by companies led to concerns about data privacy and the need for clear rules to protect individuals' information.
- More data collected means increased vulnerability to misuse, abuse, or theft, highlighting the importance of robust security measures to uphold privacy.

Notable Privacy Regulations and Compliance

- Regulations like GDPR, PCI DSS, and HIPAA are influential in shaping data handling practices and ensuring user consent and protection.
- Security audits and assessments are vital for organizations to ensure compliance with regulations and to identify and address vulnerabilities in their security posture.
