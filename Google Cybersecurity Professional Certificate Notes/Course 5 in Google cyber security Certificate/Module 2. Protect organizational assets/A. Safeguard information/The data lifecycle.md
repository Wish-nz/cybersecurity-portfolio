The Data Lifecycle

- The data lifecycle outlines how data flows within an organization, from creation to destruction, and includes stages such as collection, storage, use, archiving, and destruction.
- Protecting information at each stage ensures accessibility and recoverability in case of issues.

Data Governance and Roles

- Data governance establishes processes for managing information, including policies for privacy, accuracy, availability, and security throughout the data lifecycle.
- Key roles in data governance include data owners (decide access), data custodians (responsible for handling and storage), and data stewards (maintain and implement policies).

Legally Protected Information

- Certain types of data, such as Personally Identifiable Information (PII), Protected Health Information (PHI), and Sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (SPII), require extra protection due to their sensitive nature.
- Regulations like HIPAA and GDPR exist to ensure organizations protect these types of data by default.
