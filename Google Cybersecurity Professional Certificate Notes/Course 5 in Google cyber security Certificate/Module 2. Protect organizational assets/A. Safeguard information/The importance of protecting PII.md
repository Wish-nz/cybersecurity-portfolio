The Ubiquitous Nature of PII

- PII is an integral part of online interactions, ranging from commonly known information like names to sensitive data such as bank account numbers or medical records.
- The distinction between different types of PII is crucial because sensitive information requires more stringent protection.

Essential Security Measures for PII

- **Encryption:** Data should always be encrypted when stored ("at rest") and when transmitted over the internet, using protocols like TLS or SSL.
- **Access Control and Auditing:** Within organizations, access to sensitive PII should be highly restricted, with a clear record of who accessed it, why, and regular audits of these access logs.

Trust and User Experience

- When PII is compromised, it's vital to remember the human element and respond in a way that rebuilds trust in the systems and infrastructure.
- The ultimate goal is to ensure billions of people can safely navigate the online world every day.
