Types of Security Controls

- **Technical Controls:** These involve technologies like encryption and authentication systems to protect assets.
- **Operational Controls:** These are day-to-day security activities performed by people, such as awareness training and incident response.
- **Managerial Controls:** These focus on policies, standards, and procedures that guide how technical and operational controls reduce risk.

Information Privacy and the Principle of Least Privilege

- **Information Privacy:** This is about protecting data from unauthorized access and distribution, giving individuals and organizations the right to decide how their private information is shared.
- **Principle of Least Privilege:** Security controls should be designed to limit access to information based on the user and situation, ensuring that individuals only have access to what is necessary for their role.

Data Owners and Custodians

- **Data Owner:** This is the person who decides who can access, edit, use, or destroy their information.
- **Data Custodian:** This refers to anyone or anything (including systems) responsible for the safe handling, transport, and storage of information.
