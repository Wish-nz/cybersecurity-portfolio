PKI: A Two-Step Security Process

- PKI is an encryption framework that secures online information exchange, making access fast, easy, and secure.
- It combines asymmetric and symmetric encryption, prioritizing either speed or security depending on the situation.

Asymmetric vs. Symmetric Encryption

- **Asymmetric encryption** uses a public and private key pair; the public key encrypts data, and the private key decrypts it. This method is secure but slower.
- **Symmetric encryption** uses a single secret key for both encryption and decryption, offering faster communication but less security.

Establishing Trust with Digital Certificates

- PKI addresses the vulnerability of key sharing by using digital certificates to establish trust between computers and networks.
- A digital certificate verifies the identity of a public key holder and includes a digital signature from a trusted certificate authority (CA) to confirm its authenticity.
