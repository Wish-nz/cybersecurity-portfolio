Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

- PII encompasses any data that can identify an individual, such as names, medical records, financial details, photos, emails, or fingerprints.
- Safeguarding PII online is challenging and requires robust security measures.

Cryptography Fundamentals

- Cryptography transforms information into an unreadable format to protect it from unauthorized access.
- The process involves two main steps: encryption, which scrambles plaintext into ciphertext, and decryption, which converts ciphertext back into readable plaintext.

Caesar's Cipher: An Early Cryptographic Method

- Caesar's cipher, an ancient encryption technique, shifts letters in the alphabet by a fixed number of spaces.
- This method is vulnerable to brute-force attacks due to its limited character set and reliance on a single key, making it unsuitable for modern security needs.
