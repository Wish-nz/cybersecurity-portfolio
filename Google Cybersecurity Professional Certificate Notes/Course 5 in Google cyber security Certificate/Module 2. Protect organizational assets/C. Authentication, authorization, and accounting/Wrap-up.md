Effective Data Handling and Encryption

- **Principle of Least Privilege**: Effective data handling processes are built on this principle, ensuring individuals only have access to the information necessary for their tasks.
- **Encryption and Hashing**: These methods safeguard information, with symmetric and asymmetric encryption protecting data and hashes further securing it from harm.

Access Controls and the AAA Framework

- **Authentication and Authorization**: Properly authenticating and authorizing users is crucial for maintaining the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) of information.
- **AAA Framework**: This framework provides a detailed understanding of identity and access management systems and the access controls that verify user identities.
