**Understanding Threat Modeling**

- Threat modeling is a detailed activity typically performed by experienced professionals, considered an advanced skill in security.
- Various threat modeling frameworks exist, tailored for different security areas like network security, information security, or application development.

**Six Steps of Threat Modeling**

- **Define Scope and Identify Threats:** This involves inventorying and classifying assets, then identifying potential threat actors (internal or external) and mapping threats to assets using an attack tree.
- **Characterize Environment and Analyze Threats:** The team adopts an attacker's mindset to understand interactions with the environment, including customers, employees, and third-party vendors, then examines existing protections, identifies gaps, and ranks threats by risk score.
- **Mitigate Risk and Evaluate Findings:** The final steps involve creating a plan to defend against threats (avoid, transfer, reduce, or accept risk) and documenting the entire exercise, applying fixes, noting successes, and recording lessons learned for future threat models.
