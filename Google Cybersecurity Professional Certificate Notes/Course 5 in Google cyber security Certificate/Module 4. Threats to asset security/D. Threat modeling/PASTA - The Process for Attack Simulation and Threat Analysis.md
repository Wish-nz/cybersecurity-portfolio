Stage 1: Define Business and Security Objectives

- The initial step involves setting clear goals for the threat model, such as protecting customer data.
- This stage requires asking questions about how personally identifiable information is handled to assess the impact of potential threats.

Stage 2: Define Technical Scope & Stage 3: Decompose the Application

- The team identifies the application components that need evaluation, focusing on the attack surface, including data at rest and in use, network protocols, and security controls.
- This involves working with developers to create data flow diagrams that illustrate how data moves and the controls in place to protect it.

Stages 4, 5, and 6: Threat Analysis, Vulnerability Analysis, and Attack Modeling

- The team adopts an attacker mindset to research current attack methods relevant to mobile apps, which change regularly.
- Potential vulnerabilities are investigated in depth, and then tested by simulating attacks, often using attack trees to identify and validate threats.

Stage 7: Analyze Risk and Impact

- All information gathered from the previous stages is compiled to make informed risk management recommendations.
- These recommendations are then presented to business stakeholders, aligning with the defined business and security objectives.
