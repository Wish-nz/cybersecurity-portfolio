Exploring Major Threat Categories

- The course will begin by examining social engineering tactics, which are psychological tricks used by attackers to gain unauthorized access.
- It will then delve into malware, a common type of threat that has existed since the early days of personal computers, investigating its major types.

Web-Based Exploits and Threat Modeling

- The course will also cover web-based exploits, focusing on common online threats faced by organizations operating in the digital space.
- Finally, the section will conclude with an exploration of the threat modeling process, emphasizing its importance for security analysts.
