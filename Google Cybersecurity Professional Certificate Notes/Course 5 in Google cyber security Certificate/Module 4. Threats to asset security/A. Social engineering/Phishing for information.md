Phishing Kits: Tools of the Trade

- Phishing kits are collections of software tools that enable individuals with limited technical expertise to launch phishing campaigns.
- These kits commonly include malicious attachments, fake data collection forms, and fraudulent web links designed to mimic legitimate sources and steal information.

Evolving Phishing Techniques

- Beyond traditional email phishing, cybercriminals now employ "smishing" (SMS phishing) and "vishing" (voice phishing) to trick individuals into divulging private information.
- Smishing messages can be difficult to detect, sometimes using local area codes or impersonating known contacts, while vishing involves criminals posing as company representatives to solicit sensitive data.

Organizational Defenses Against Phishing

- Organizations implement anti-phishing policies and employee training to raise awareness and promote secure data handling practices.
- Technical measures like email filters (blocklists and allowlists) and intrusion prevention systems are used to secure inboxes, identify suspicious email patterns, and quarantine malicious messages.
