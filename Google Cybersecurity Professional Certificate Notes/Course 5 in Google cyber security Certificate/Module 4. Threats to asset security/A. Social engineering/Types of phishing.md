The Origins and Evolution of Phishing

- Phishing emerged in the 1990s with the rise of the internet, as malicious actors exploited anonymity to trick users of services like AOL Instant Messenger (AIM) into revealing personal information.
- Early attacks involved crafting fake emails that mimicked official communications, using logos, colors, and fonts to deceive users.

Common Phishing Tactics

- **Email phishing** involves threat actors sending deceptive emails pretending to be trusted entities.
- **Smishing** utilizes text messages (SMS) and other chat mediums to trick individuals.
- **Vishing** uses voice calls or messages to manipulate targets into providing personal information.
- **Spear phishing** targets specific individuals, while **whaling** specifically targets high-ranking executives.

Recent Trends and Key Takeaways

- Phishing has evolved from mass attacks to more targeted approaches, such as angler phishing, where attackers impersonate customer service on social media to exploit customer complaints.
- While there's no perfect solution to prevent phishing, increasing awareness and educating others, especially as a security professional, can help mitigate the damage from these attacks.
