Network Traffic Fundamentals

- **Definition**: Network traffic refers to the amount of data moving across a network, while network data is the information transmitted between devices.
- **Volume**: Large organizations can generate significant network traffic, making it crucial to distinguish between normal and unusual activity.

Identifying Abnormal Network Behavior

- **Expected vs. Abnormal**: Similar to traffic patterns on a road, understanding typical network traffic flow helps identify anomalies.
- **Indicators of Compromise (IoC)**: These are observable pieces of evidence that suggest a potential security incident, such as data exfiltration.

Data Exfiltration

- **Unauthorized Transmission**: Data exfiltration is the unauthorized transfer of data from a system, often involving sensitive information like usernames or intellectual property.
- **Detection**: Large volumes of outbound network traffic can be an indicator of data exfiltration, prompting further investigation by security professionals.
