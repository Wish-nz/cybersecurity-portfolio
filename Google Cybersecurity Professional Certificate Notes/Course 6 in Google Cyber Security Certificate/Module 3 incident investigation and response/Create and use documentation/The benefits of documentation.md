Enhances Transparency and Evidence

- **Record-keeping:** Documentation creates a record of events, making relevant information accessible.
- **Source of Evidence:** Transparent documentation serves as crucial evidence for security insurance claims, regulatory investigations, and legal proceedings.

Promotes Standardization and Clarity

- **Established Guidelines:** Documentation provides standardized guidelines for tasks and workflows, ensuring consistent quality of work.
- **Improved Understanding:** Effective documentation clarifies roles, duties, and procedures, reducing uncertainty during incident response.

Supports Continuous Improvement

- **Regular Maintenance:** Documentation needs regular review and updates to adapt to evolving threats and regulatory changes.
- **Personal and Organizational Value:** Documenting actions helps security professionals recall facts, identify gaps, and provides valuable information for the entire organization.
