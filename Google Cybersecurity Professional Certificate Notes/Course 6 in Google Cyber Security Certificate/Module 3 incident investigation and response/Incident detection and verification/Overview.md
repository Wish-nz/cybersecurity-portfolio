**Understanding the Security Incident Lifecycle**

- You will learn how security incidents are detected, responded to, and recovered from.
- The section will cover the entire process, from the initial detection to post-incident actions.

**Key Areas of Focus**

- You will investigate and verify incidents once they have been detected.
- The course will explore the plans and processes involved in effective incident response.

**Learning and Improvement**

- You will learn about the actions organizations take after an incident to learn from the experience and improve their security posture.
- The goal is to gain a comprehensive understanding of an incident's lifecycle.
