Playbooks as Incident Response Guides

- Playbooks are manuals that provide detailed instructions for security analysts to follow during an incident, offering structure and reducing uncertainty.
- They outline steps for various attacks, such as ransomware or DDoS, and can include checklists to ensure all necessary actions are completed.

Types of Playbooks

- **Non-automated playbooks** require analysts to perform each step manually, as demonstrated by the DDoS playbook example.
- **Automated playbooks** can handle tasks like incident severity categorization or evidence gathering, speeding up resolution times and often integrating with SOAR and SIEM tools.
- **Semi-automated playbooks** combine human action with automation, allowing analysts to focus on critical tasks while automating tedious ones, thereby increasing productivity.

Maintaining Playbook Effectiveness

- Playbooks must be regularly updated and maintained to remain effective against evolving threats.
- The post-incident activity phase is an ideal time to introduce necessary changes and improvements to playbooks.
