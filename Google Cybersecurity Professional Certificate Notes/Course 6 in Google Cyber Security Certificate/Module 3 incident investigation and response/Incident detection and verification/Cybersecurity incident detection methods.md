Methods of Detection

- Security teams use detection tools like Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) and Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) to identify and investigate suspicious activity.
- Challenges exist with detection tools, as they only detect what they are configured to monitor, highlighting the need for additional detection methods.

Threat Hunting

- Threat hunting is a proactive, human-driven approach to search for hidden threats that automated tools might miss, such as fileless malware.
- Threat hunters use a combination of threat intelligence, indicators of compromise, indicators of attack, and machine learning to identify potential vulnerabilities.

Threat Intelligence and Cyber Deception

- Threat intelligence provides context about existing or emerging threats through industry reports, government advisories, and threat data feeds.
- Cyber deception, such as using honeypots, deliberately misleads malicious actors to increase detection and improve defensive strategies.
