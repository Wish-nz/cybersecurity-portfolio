## The Concept of Triage

- Triage is a process used to categorize and prioritize incidents based on their urgency and importance.
- In cybersecurity, triage helps security teams manage limited resources by focusing on the most critical threats first.

## Triage in Cybersecurity

- Security incidents are triaged based on the threat they pose to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems.
- For example, a ransomware attack would be prioritized over an employee receiving a phishing email due to its potential for significant damage.

## The Triage Process

- Upon receiving an alert, a security analyst assesses if it's a false positive or related to an existing incident.
- If it's a true positive, the alert is assigned a priority level based on organizational policies, and then investigated to gather and analyze evidence.

## The triage process in cybersecurity involves several steps once an incident is detected and an alert is sent out. Here's a breakdown:

1. **Receive and Assess the Alert**: You'll first receive the alert and determine if it's a **false positive** or if it's related to an **existing incident**.
2. **Assign Priority**: If it's a true positive, you'll assign a priority level to the alert based on your organization's policies and guidelines. This priority level dictates how the security team will respond.
3. **Investigate and Collect Evidence**: Finally, you'll investigate the alert and gather evidence, such as system logs. This thorough analysis helps you make an informed decision about your findings.
