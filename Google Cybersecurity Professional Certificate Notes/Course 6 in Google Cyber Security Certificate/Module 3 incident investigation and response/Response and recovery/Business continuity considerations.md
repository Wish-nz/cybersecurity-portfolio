Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

- A BCP is a documented plan outlining procedures to sustain business operations during and after significant disruptions, helping to quickly restore critical functions.
- While entry-level security analysts may not develop BCPs, understanding their role in structured incident response and recovery is crucial.

Impacts of Security Incidents

- Security incidents like ransomware can severely disrupt business operations, potentially affecting critical infrastructure and essential services.
- Such disruptions can lead to legal, financial, and reputational damages, and in larger contexts, can undermine national security and public safety.

Recovery Strategies

- BCPs include recovery strategies to return to normal operations after an outage caused by a security incident.
- Site resilience, which involves designing systems to continue delivering services despite disruptions, utilizes hot, warm, and cold sites for recovery.
