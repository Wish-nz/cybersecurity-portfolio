Containment: Limiting Incident Damage

- Containment is the initial step after detection, aiming to prevent further damage and limit the spread of an incident.
- Organizations develop specific containment strategies within their incident response plans, such as isolating affected systems to prevent malware propagation.

Eradication: Removing Incident Elements

- Once contained, eradication involves completely removing all traces of the incident from affected systems.
- This includes actions like performing vulnerability tests and applying patches to address the vulnerabilities exploited by the threat.

Recovery: Restoring Normal Operations

- Recovery is the final step, focusing on returning affected systems and services to their normal operational state.
- Recovery actions can involve reimaging systems, resetting passwords, and adjusting network configurations like firewall rules.
