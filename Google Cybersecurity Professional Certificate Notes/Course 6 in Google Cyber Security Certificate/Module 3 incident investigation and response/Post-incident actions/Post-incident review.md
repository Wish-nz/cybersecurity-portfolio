Post-incident Activity and Lessons Learned

- The post-incident phase involves reviewing an incident to identify areas for improvement in incident handling.
- "Lessons learned" meetings, or post-mortems, are crucial for organizations to learn from incidents and improve their response processes, focusing on improvement rather than blame.

Recommendations and Final Report

- These meetings should result in prioritized actions or recommendations to enhance incident handling and overall security, such as updating playbooks or implementing new security tools.
- A final report, a comprehensive review of the incident, should be created, detailing the "who, what, where, why, and when" of the incident, and tailored to its audience.

## Examples of questions that are addressed in this meeting:

- What happened?
    
- What time did it happen?
    
- Who discovered it?
    
- How did it get contained?
    
- What were the actions taken for recovery?
    
- What could have been done differently?
