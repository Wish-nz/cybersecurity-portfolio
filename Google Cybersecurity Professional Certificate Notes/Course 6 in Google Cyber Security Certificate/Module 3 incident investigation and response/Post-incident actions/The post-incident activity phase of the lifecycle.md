Post-Incident Activity Phase

- This phase involves reviewing an incident to identify areas for improvement in handling future incidents.
- It includes updating or creating various types of documentation, such as the final report.

The Final Report

- The final report provides a comprehensive review of an incident, detailing the timeline, events, and recommendations for future prevention.
- It serves as a crucial reference document during lessons learned meetings.

Lessons Learned Meetings

- These meetings involve all parties involved in an incident to discuss what happened, actions taken, and their effectiveness.
- The goal is to share ideas and information to improve future response efforts, focusing on learning and improvement rather than assigning blame.
