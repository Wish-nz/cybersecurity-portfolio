Incident Lifecycle Frameworks

- Incident lifecycle frameworks, like the NIST CSF, provide a structured approach to incident response, ensuring consistent and effective management of security incidents.
- The NIST CSF has five core functions: identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover. This course will focus on the detect, respond, and recover phases.

NIST Incident Response Lifecycle

- The NIST incident response lifecycle includes preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication and recovery, and post-incident activity.
- This lifecycle is not linear; steps can overlap as new information emerges, providing a blueprint for effective incident response.

Understanding Security Incidents

- An incident is defined by NIST as an occurrence that jeopardizes the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or an information system, or violates security policies.
- All security incidents are events (observable occurrences), but not all events are security incidents. Security analysts investigate incidents to determine the "five W's": who, what, when, where, and why, documenting their findings in an incident handler's journal.
