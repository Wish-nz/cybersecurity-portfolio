Command, Control, and Communication in CSIRTs

- **Effective Incident Response:** For incident response to be effective, clear command, control, and communication are essential to manage challenges and achieve desired outcomes.
- **Key Elements:** Command provides leadership, control manages technical aspects and resources, and communication keeps stakeholders informed throughout the incident.

Roles within CSIRTs

- **Varied Structures:** CSIRTs are organization-dependent and can vary in structure, involving both security and non-security professionals.
- **Core Security Roles:** Key security roles typically include the Security Analyst (monitoring and triaging threats), Technical Lead (managing technical aspects and recovery), and Incident Coordinator (cross-departmental communication).

Security Operations Center (SOC)

- **Purpose:** A SOC is a dedicated unit for monitoring networks, systems, and devices to detect security threats and attacks, often involved in "blue team" activities.
- **Tiered Structure:** SOCs are typically composed of SOC Analysts (tiered by experience for monitoring, investigation, and escalation), SOC Leads (managing operations and advanced detection), and SOC Managers (overseeing the team and reporting).
