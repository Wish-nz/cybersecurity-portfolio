The Purpose of CSIRTs

- CSIRTs are specialized teams of security professionals trained to manage incidents effectively and efficiently.
- Their main goals include providing services for response and recovery, and preventing future incidents.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Security is a shared responsibility, requiring CSIRTs to collaborate with other departments like legal and public relations.
- This collaboration ensures proper handling of sensitive data breaches and coordinated public disclosure when necessary.

CSIRT Roles and Functions

- **Security Analyst:** Investigates security alerts, determines if an incident has occurred, and assesses its criticality.
- **Technical Lead:** Provides technical guidance for highly critical incidents throughout their lifecycle.
- **Incident Coordinator:** Tracks and manages CSIRT activities, ensuring adherence to response processes and keeping teams updated.
