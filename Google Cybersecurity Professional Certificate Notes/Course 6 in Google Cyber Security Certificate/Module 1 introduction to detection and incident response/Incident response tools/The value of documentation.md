What is Documentation?

- Documentation is any recorded content serving a specific purpose, which can include audio, digital, handwritten instructions, and videos.
- There is no universal industry standard; organizations often customize their documentation practices to meet their specific needs and legal requirements.

Types of Documentation

- Common types of documentation include playbooks, incident handler's journals, policies, plans, and final reports.
- Playbooks, similar to product manuals, provide detailed instructions for operational actions, crucial for maintaining business safety.

Importance of Effective Documentation

- Effective documentation is critical for reducing uncertainty and confusion, especially during high-tension security incidents.
- It must be clear, consistent, and accurate to enable security professionals to respond swiftly and decisively.
