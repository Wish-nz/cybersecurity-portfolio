Parking lot USB exercise

Scenario

Evaluate the situation below and follow the subsequent procedural guidelines.
As a member of the Rhetorical Hospital security department, you discover a USB thumb drive bearing the corporate logo while traversing the parking area. Since no potential owners are in the vicinity, you recover the hardware to investigate.
The device is transported to a designated security workstation equipped with virtualization tools. Utilizing a virtual machine is a primary defense for examining unknown storage media, as it creates an isolated sandbox environment. This ensures that any potential malware cannot propagate to internal networks or local file systems during the forensic analysis.
