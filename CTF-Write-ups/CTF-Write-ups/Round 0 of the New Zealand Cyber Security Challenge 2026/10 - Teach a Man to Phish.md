What to check first
Open the .eml in a text editor, not a mail client.

Inspect these fields first:

From
Reply-To
Return-Path
Received
Message-ID
any X- headers.

Search the body for:

URLs.
tracking pixels.
display-name tricks.
attached or inline content.

Look for hidden clues in:

HTML comments.
base64 blobs.
unusual domains.
phone numbers, names, usernames, or addresses.
